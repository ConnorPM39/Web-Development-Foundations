Our Mission.

At Twin Cities Animal Rescue, we believe every companion animal deserves a safe haven and a loving lifelong home.

Through our community networks, and our accessible adoption programs, we bridge the gap between lovable animals and caring families across the Twin Cities metro area.

Take a look at what we've accomplished so far!

  • 1,450+ Animals successfully placed into permanent loving homes.
  • 320+ Active volunteer foster homes providing temporary shelter and care.
  • 100% Of rescued animals receive full veterinary exams, vaccinations, and spay/neuter services before adoption.
  • 98% Placement success rate across Minneapolis, St. Paul, and surrounding communities.

How you can help.

I'm sure you are wondering what you can do to help make a difference, and we assure you that it doesn't take much!

Every positive outcome starts with a neighbor willing to step up.

Whether you open your living room to a temporary foster pet, dedicate weekend hours to transport and events, or give a tax-deductible donation, your action directly saves lives right here in Minnesota.

  • Foster: Provide a safe resting spot while an animal waits for their forever family.
  • Adopt: Meet your new best friend and give a rescued pet a fresh start.
  • Volunteer: Lend your time, skills, or vehicle to keep rescue operations moving forward.
  • Donate: Any amount given will allow us to continue doing what we do, and is a step forward in providing a home to each of our animals.
