Let's talk about our Adoption and Foster programs!

Adoption Process

Finding your match should be exciting, transparent, and supportive from day one

Here are some easy steps

  1. Browse & Apply: Review our available dogs and cats online, then submit a standard adoption application.
  2. Conversation & Match: Our adoption coordinators discuss your lifestyle, home setup, and expectations to ensure a safe, lasting fit.
  3. Meet & Greet: Arrange an in-person or foster-home meeting with the animal.
  4. Finalize Adoption: Complete the adoption agreement, pay the adoption fee (covering spay/neuter, microchip, and core vaccines), and bring your new companion home.

Foster Program

Foster families form the backbone of our rescue because we operate entirely without a traditional kennel facility.
When you foster, you give a dog or cat a calm home environment to decompress and learn social skills.
We provide all the essentials—food, medical care, crates, leashes, and behavioral guidance—while you provide the roof, love, and daily routine until an adoption is finalized.

Volunteer Roles:

  • Transport Driver: Pick up animals from regional partner shelters or transport them to veterinary appointments across the metro area.
  • Adoption Event Assistant: Help set up weekend adoption showcases, handle animals, greet visitors, and answer initial questions from the public.
  • Administrative & Application Support: Review incoming foster/adoption inquiries, perform vet reference checks, and assist with digital records from home.
  • Community Outreach & Supply Coordinator: Help organize donation drives, pick up pet food deliveries, and assemble foster supply kits.

To sign up for one of these options, or to reach out about volunteering, visit our "Contact Us" page.
